## Product Purpose

A concept demo by Deralis Digital: one operations screen for a field-service company. It answers three
questions in one place: which missions are running or coming up, which one needs a decision now
(an incident on site), and which agent is free to respond. The manager opens the incident, hands it to an
available agent, closes it, and assigns new client requests.

Success: a prospect understands in seconds that incidents surface by themselves and can be dispatched and
closed in a few clicks, without phone calls or a spreadsheet.

## Positioning

Built around the exception, not the report: the screen leads with what needs action (open incidents,
unassigned requests) and the people who can take it, rather than with charts of past activity.

## Operating Context

Fictional company "Rempart Sécurité" (inferred name for the demo). Five fictional clients (property,
laboratory, logistics, private clinic, wealth management). 24 missions seeded relative to today, eight
agents with duty status. All data lives in the visitor's browser and can be reset.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: `/` start page, `/dashboard`, `/jobs` (missions), `/team` (équipe). URLs kept from the original.
- All UI copy in French (fr-FR dates and times). No em dashes in copy.
- No real telephony, SMS, radio or client notifications: nothing is sent. Never imply otherwise.
- No public side: the product is an internal back office only.
- One business configuration (private security). No sector switcher.

## Evidence on Hand

No real clients, agents, testimonials, metrics or photographs. Every name and figure is sample data.

## Product Principles

1. The exception leads: what needs a decision is read first.
2. Every incident has an owner and a next action visible on screen.
3. People are shown as people: availability and current load next to every name.
4. Honest demo: fictional data labelled, nothing simulated presented as live.
